## What makes or breaks it

- **The price is the business.** At $589 there's $400 of contribution to fund customer acquisition and the packaging theater. At $345 the model roughly halves.
- **CAC is the biggest swing.** $120 blended assumes strong organic/gifting momentum; at $240 CAC, EBITDA drops to ~$1M.
- **Demand is the real risk, not margin.** $8.8M of demand for one SKU needs real brand heat. Honest year one: 3,000–5,000 units, scale on a proven waitlist (see Scenarios tab).
- **Working capital:** German mill batches on 8–12 week lead times tie up $0.7–1M mid-cycle — the numbered annual-run model pre-finances production, not just psychology.
