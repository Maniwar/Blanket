Privacy, plainly

Effective July 4, 2026

Concept demonstration Feierabend is a design study. No real product exists, no payment is taken, and nothing ships.
01 — What we collect
  • Name, email address, city and state — when you enter the demo register.
  • Anonymous chat messages you type to the concierge, logged to improve answers.
  • No payment data, ever. None is requested.
  • No cookies for advertising. Functional storage only — session preferences and sign-in.
02 — Why

To hold your number in the demo register, to answer you when signed in — and nothing else.

Data minimization is the principle here, not an afterthought. We don't ask for street addresses or phone numbers because a demonstration doesn't need them.

03 — What we never do
  • Sell personal information.
  • Share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.
  • Run third-party ad trackers.

Because we neither sell nor share, California law does not require an opt-out link on this site. Global Privacy Control signals are respected by default all the same — there is simply nothing to opt out of.

04 — Where it lives

Register entries are stored with Supabase (hosted Postgres, US region). Concierge chat is processed by Anthropic's Claude API. Both operate under their respective processor terms and handle data only on our instructions.

05 — Your rights (California and everyone else)

You have the right to know what we hold about you, to access it, to correct it, and to delete it. Exercising any of these will never change how we treat you — non-discrimination is the rule.

One email to hello@feierabend.example handles any of it. We verify requests simply: reply from the address on file. We answer within 45 days, as the CCPA asks.

An authorized agent may submit a request on your behalf; we will confirm with you before acting on it.

06 — Retention

Register entries and chat logs are kept only while the demonstration runs. They are deleted on request, or when the study ends — whichever comes first.

07 — Changes & contact

This notice is effective July 4, 2026. If the demonstration changes in a way that touches your data, this page will say so before the change does anything. Questions, corrections, deletions: hello@feierabend.example.

Read less than you think — there is simply not much collected.
